## 📌 STEP 4: CUSTOM DOMAIN SETUP (VERCEL)

To attach your branded domain (e.g., `vendorshield.com` or `snapinspect.com`):

1. Buy your domain on Namecheap, Cloudflare, or Google Domains.
2. Go to **[Vercel Dashboard → vendorshield → Settings → Domains](https://vercel.com/rrcc1/vendorshield/settings/domains)**.
3. Enter your domain (e.g. `vendorshield.com`).
4. Set DNS Records at your registrar:
   - **Type:** `A` | **Name:** `@` | **Value:** `76.76.21.21`
   - **Type:** `CNAME` | **Name:** `www` | **Value:** `cname.vercel-dns.com`
5. Vercel will automatically generate a free SSL certificate within 60 seconds!
