2026-09-20. Draft only: no messages sent, purchases made, prices changed, or billing enabled. These are hypotheses, not customers or confirmed buying interest. Narrowed from `PROSPECT_SHORTLIST_2026-09-20.md` using first-party pages checked today.

## Offer hypothesis

**$199 one-time for a manually assisted setup pilot**, with **$29/month ongoing access/support only as a later pricing question**. Both prices are unvalidated hypotheses, not current published plans or agreed terms. Do not create a charge or promise paid access until delivery, billing, support terms and customer consent are ready.

Scope: one organization, one owner, up to 15 customer-approved vendors; manually organize vendor names, processing category/region, DPA links and status; publish one standard public register, provide its iframe snippet; produce PDF/CSV/JSON record exports; one 30-minute handoff and one correction round within seven days. Target delivery: two business days after complete authorized inputs, subject to a dry run proving the workflow.

Acceptance: customer checks all 15 records; owner changes one record; updated public page is correct after refresh; exports match the approved register; iframe works on their chosen page. Label unknowns rather than inventing certifications, signatures or review dates.

Excluded: legal advice, compliance or audit guarantees, automated monitoring, independent verification, automatic notices/alerts, invitation email/acceptance, custom domains, multi-client administration, customer-specific integrations, and all Dispel/SnapInspect functionality. No claim that exports are digitally signed or independently attested. Do not request sensitive contracts via a generic sales form.

## Concrete next steps after separate outreach authorization

1. Start with the two referral partners and Nango. Ask for the correct role rather than treating sales staff as buyers. Do not mass-send through customer-support forms.
2. Offer a ten-minute walkthrough using clearly labeled sample data. Ask what they currently do, where it fails, who owns the task, and whether they would pay for the bounded setup.
3. Record replies as evidence: no problem / existing tool / wrong role / interested / willing to pay. Interest is not a sale; an agreed paid pilot is not recurring SaaS revenue.
4. If a prospect accepts scope, run the acceptance checklist before requesting payment through a separately authorized, working billing flow. Do not re-enable the old broad subscription claims.
5. Stop expanding the product until one real operator completes the core workflow and confirms it is useful. After five conversations, revise the offer from observed objections rather than building speculative features.
