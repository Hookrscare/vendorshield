# VendorShield Prospect Shortlist

Updated: 2026-09-20

These are public prospects and referral-partner candidates, not existing VendorShield customers. Pain points are hypotheses inferred from first-party public evidence. Public sales, demo, and contact pages are routing clues only; they do not establish the correct procurement owner or confirm buying interest. No one was contacted.

## Product-origin caveat

Paid-pilot promises must stay within the capabilities currently verified in this repository:

- A vendor register that records data categories, hosting region, DPA URL/status, notes, and risk rating.
- Directory-assisted entry for the vendors already covered by VendorShield's 30+ profile directory.
- A public portal and embeddable subprocessor widget backed by the workspace register.
- Branded PDF plus CSV and JSON auditor exports.
- A workspace change trail for vendor additions, edits, and DPA-status changes.

Do **not** promise automated external monitoring or scraping of vendor DPAs, certifications, or Trust Centers; automatic customer email notices; prospect-specific integrations; multi-region or multi-client partner administration; or guaranteed audit/compliance outcomes. Those are future capabilities unless separately implemented and verified, and they are excluded from the paid pilots below.

## Recommended validation sequence

1. **Nango:** strongest combination of visible manual register work and achievable standard-product pilot.
2. **Linear:** strong fit, but validate whether its existing Trust Center/notification workflow leaves a real gap before proposing paid work.
3. **Rhymetec and Com-Sec:** validate interest in a single-client referral pilot before discussing channel economics or partner tooling.
4. **Langfuse:** approach only with a narrow regional/high-risk evidence use case; avoid positioning VendorShield as a replacement for its mature security program.
5. **Clerk, Cal.com, and Retool:** keep as lower-priority learning conversations or design partners because incumbents reduce likely purchase intent.

If outreach is later authorized, it should show a non-confidential mockup of the prospect's public register represented in VendorShield and offer a bounded setup/export pilot. It must not imply that public form recipients are procurement owners, promise unverified automation, or claim that VendorShield guarantees compliance or an audit result.
