## Recommended first outreach sequence

1. Nango: clearest public register and change-feed replacement story.
2. Langfuse: strongest region-specific AI-compliance use case.
3. Cal.com: broad regulated-market proof point.
4. Rhymetec and Com-Sec: channel leverage across multiple startup clients.

Any outreach should lead with a specific 14-day pilot and a screenshot of the prospect's own current public register transformed into VendorShield, not a generic compliance pitch.
