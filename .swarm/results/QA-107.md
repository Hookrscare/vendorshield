## Technical Accomplishments & Business Impact

### 1. Multi-Tenant Team Seats & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- **Roster & Permission Center (`/dashboard/team`)**: Workspace dashboard featuring active team members, role badges (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Member`, `Viewer`), invitation statuses, and direct access controls.
- **Strict Server-Side Authorization (`/api/team`)**:
  - `POST /api/team`: Enforces owner/admin authorization to create coworker invitations. Viewers and members receive 403 Forbidden.
  - `DELETE /api/team`: Enforces owner/admin role for removing team members and guards against removing the last owner.
- **Anonymous Demo Isolation**: Anonymous users access a sanitized, read-only demo team roster (`isDemo: true`) with mutation actions safely blocked.

### 2. Durable Stripe Webhook Idempotency & Entitlement Activation
- **Idempotency Ledger (`public.stripe_events`)**:
  - Inbound webhook events check `stripe_events.event_id`.
  - Duplicate webhook deliveries return `{ received: true, deduplicated: true }` without re-running billing side effects.
- **Entitlement Provisioning (`public.entitlements`)**:
  - When `checkout.session.completed` arrives, the purchasing organization receives an atomic upsert into `public.entitlements` bound to their `organizationId`.
  - Upgrades from `free` (15 vendor limit) to `startup` (50 vendor limit) or `growth` (1,000 vendor limit) take effect immediately across all workspace sessions.

### 3. Dynamic Plan Status
- Header displays active plan status badge (`Plan: Startup`, `Plan: Growth`, or `Community Tier`).
- Company settings API (`/api/company`) automatically returns the current workspace plan and feature limits.
