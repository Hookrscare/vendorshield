## Deliverables & Business Impact

### 1. Directory Profile Monetization ($199/mo)
- Every programmatic SEO vendor profile (`/directory/[vendorSlug]`) now passes its unique `vendorSlug` through `CheckoutButton` into `/api/checkout`.
- Safe custom metadata mapping ensures that upon checkout completion, Stripe webhooks can provision the verified profile status for the claiming vendor.

### 2. GDPR Article 28(2) Sub-Processor Change Alerts
- Public disclosure register (`/p/[slug]`) features a prominent "Subscribe to Change Alerts" modal.
- Enterprise customers and auditors can register their corporate security email to receive advance notifications before any vendor is added, changed, or replaced.
- Handled safely via `POST /api/public/[slug]/subscribe` with resilient fallback behavior for demo registers.
